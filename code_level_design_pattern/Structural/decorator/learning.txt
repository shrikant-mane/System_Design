Decorator is a structural design pattern that dynamically adds responsibilities or
behavior to an object without modifying its original class.

The important words are:
Structural → dynamically → add behavior → without modifying original class


========================

Think about ordering a coffee.

Start with:
Coffee
₹100

Add milk:
Coffee
  +
Milk
₹120

Add sugar:
Coffee
  +
Milk
  +
Sugar
₹130

Add whipped cream:
Coffee
  +
Milk
  +
Sugar
  +
Whipped Cream
₹160

You don't create:
CoffeeWithMilk
CoffeeWithMilkAndSugar
CoffeeWithMilkSugarAndCream

Instead, you decorate the coffee.
WhippedCream
     ↓
   Sugar
     ↓
    Milk
     ↓
   Coffee

This is the core idea behind the Decorator Pattern.
